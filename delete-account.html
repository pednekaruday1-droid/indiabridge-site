India Bridge — Delete your account and data

App: India Bridge (Android app, package com.indiabridge.app)  |  Contact: dairyindiabridge@gmail.com

The India Bridge directory needs no account. The only account in the app is the optional India Bridge Diary account (email address and password). You can delete this account, and everything saved in it, yourself in the app, or ask us by email.

Option 1: delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open India Bridge and open the Diary. Sign in if you are asked to.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) icon at the top.
  3. Tap Delete My Account. (You can also open My Diary Account and tap Delete My Account there.)
  4. Type your Diary password and tap Delete My Account Permanently.

The deletion happens at once and cannot be undone. If you forgot your password: on the Diary sign-in page tap Reset Password, set a new password from the email we send you, sign in, and follow the steps above.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you cannot open the app (for example you uninstalled it or lost your phone), write to dairyindiabridge@gmail.com from the email address of your Diary account, with the subject Delete my India Bridge Diary account. We delete the account and its data and confirm by reply, within 30 days (usually much sooner). You do not need to install the app again.

What is deleted

  • Your Diary account: your email address and password sign-in.
  • Everything saved in your Diary in our cloud database (Google Firebase): birthdays, anniversaries, appointments, reminders, contact details, notes and every other entry, the Trash, and the parts of very long entries.
  • On the phone where you delete it from the app: that account's Diary entries, Trash, drafts, settings, PIN lock and alarms.

What is not deleted, and how long things are kept

  • Bazaar listings are not linked to your email, because posting uses an anonymous sign-in. Remove your listings in the app under Kisan & Wholesale Bazaar, My Listings. Or write to us with the item name and the phone number you used, and we remove the listing.
  • Backup files that you saved yourself (Diary, Settings, Backup & Transfer) stay wherever you saved them. Please delete them yourself.
  • When data is deleted from the cloud database, Google may keep short-lived technical backup copies for a limited time before they are erased automatically. We keep no other copy of your Diary.

More details are in our Privacy Policy.
