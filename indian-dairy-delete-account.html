Indian Dairy — Delete your account and data

This page explains how to delete your Indian Dairy account (app by Grotech Global) and your diary data, and what happens to it.

Last updated: November 2026

Option 1: Delete inside the app (instant)

  1. Open Indian Dairy and sign in.
  2. Tap Settings, then Privacy & Consent, then Delete Account & Data.
  3. Type your current password, tap Delete My Account & Data and confirm.

Want to keep a copy first? Save a backup in Settings → Backup & Transfer. Deleting cannot be undone.

Option 2: Ask us by email (if you uninstalled the app or cannot sign in)

Send an email to indiabridgediary@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Indian Dairy account.

  • Write from the email address you used to sign up, or write that address in your email.
  • Do not send your password.

We delete the data within 30 days (usually much sooner) and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email address and password).
  • All your diary entries and your Trash in the cloud (Google Firebase).
  • When you delete inside the app: the entries, alarms and settings of that account on that phone.

What is not deleted

We do not keep a copy of deleted data. Google, our cloud provider, may hold routine backup copies for a limited time before they are permanently erased. Backup files you exported yourself, and copies you saved or shared elsewhere, are yours: please remove them yourself.
