Indian Dairy — Privacy Policy

Indian Dairy is a private diary and reminder app by Grotech Global (India Bridge apps). This page explains what information the app uses, where it is kept and how you can delete it.

Last updated: November 2026 · Contact: indiabridgediary@gmail.com

In short
  • You sign in with an email address and a password. Your diary entries stay on your phone and, when you are signed in, in your own private cloud space (Google Firebase).
  • There are no ads and no analytics. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with other companies.
  • You can delete your account and all your cloud data yourself, inside the app, at any time.

Information the app uses

  • Account: your email address and password. The password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we cannot read it.
  • Diary entries: what you type into the cards — Birthdays, Anniversaries, Schedule & Appointments, Daily Tasks, Reminders & Alarms, Contact Details, Personal Details, Notes and Favorites — with the dates, times, names, phone numbers and other details you choose to write, plus your Trash.
  • Settings: your language, appearance and privacy choices, stored on your phone.

The app does not read your phone's contacts, call log, messages, photos, location, camera or microphone. “Contact Details” is only a diary card where you type details yourself.

Where your entries are kept

Entries are saved on your phone. When you are signed in, a copy is also saved in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud, Mumbai region) under your own account, so your diary is not lost if you change or lose your phone. Firebase security rules allow only your signed-in account to read or write its own entries. Connections to Firebase use HTTPS. Diary text is not end-to-end encrypted, so please protect your phone and your password.

Reminders, alarms and notifications

Reminders and alarms are scheduled on your phone itself. The app asks for permission to show notifications, to set exact alarms and to show a full-screen alert so that a reminder is not missed. If you decline, the app keeps working, but a reminder may appear as an ordinary notification or not appear at all.

Google services

  • Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore (Google) run the sign-in and the cloud copy, as described above. The app loads Firebase code from Google's servers when you are online.
  • Google Translate (optional): when you are online the app can load Google's translation tool. Google may receive technical details such as your IP address and device or browser details. If you use translation, the text visible on the screen is processed by Google. Do not use translation on text you do not want Google to process.

Ads, analytics and payments

Indian Dairy has no ads and no advertising or analytics service. The “Privacy Choices” switches in Settings are stored only on your phone; this version has no analytics service they could switch on. This version has no paid features and does not take payments.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with other companies. The only outside services are the Google services listed above.

Delete your data

Open Settings → Privacy & Consent → Delete Account & Data, type your password and confirm. This deletes your sign-in and all your cloud diary entries and Trash. You can also ask by email. Full steps: Delete your account and data.

How long data is kept

Your data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Backup files you export yourself, and copies you shared or saved elsewhere, stay with you until you remove them. Google may keep routine backup copies for a limited time before they are permanently erased.

Children

Indian Dairy is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change we will update this page and its date. Questions: indiabridgediary@gmail.com.
