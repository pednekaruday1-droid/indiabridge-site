IB World FM — Privacy Policy

IB World FM is an internet radio app by Grotech Global (India Bridge apps). You can listen without an account. This page explains what the app uses, where it is kept and how you can delete it.

Last updated: November 2026 · Contact: ibfmworld@gmail.com

In short
  • No account is needed to listen. An account is optional and only used to sync your favourites between phones.
  • There are no ads, no analytics and no payments in the app. We do not sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and its cloud data yourself, inside the app, at any time.

On your phone

Your favourites, custom stations, station order, sound settings, language, appearance and the last station you played are stored on your phone. Android may back up app data according to your device backup settings. “Clear Cache” removes temporary files, not your favourites or account.

Optional account and cloud sync

If you create an account or sign in, Google Firebase Authentication processes your email address, your password and your account ID. If you use cloud sync, Google Firebase Cloud Firestore stores your favourites, custom stations, station order and selected settings in a private space under your account. This Firebase project belongs to IB World FM only and is not shared with other apps. Firebase security rules allow only your signed-in account to read or write its own data, and these connections use HTTPS. We cannot read your password.

Radio stations and network connections

To show stations and play audio, the app connects to radio directories (such as radio-browser.info), to the stations' own streaming servers and to station logo or artwork providers. These providers receive technical details such as your IP address and may have their own privacy policies. Some radio streams use unencrypted HTTP. Which stations are available depends on the providers.

Language translation

If you choose Hindi or Marathi, some words shown in the app that are not built in (for example station descriptions or messages) are sent to Google's online translation service to be translated. Your email address and password are never sent for translation.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: load stations, play streams and sync your account.
  • Foreground service (media playback), wake lock and notifications: keep the radio playing with the screen off and show playback controls.
  • Audio settings: equalizer and sound effects.

The app does not ask for your contacts, microphone, camera, photos or location.

Ads, analytics and payments

The app has no ads, no analytics service and no payments. Radio stations may play their own advertisements inside their broadcasts.

Delete your data

Open Settings → Login & Firebase Sync, sign in, type your password in the Password box and tap Delete Account. This deletes your sign-in and your cloud data. You can also ask by email. Full steps: Delete your account and data.

How long data is kept

Cloud data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Data on your phone stays until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Google may keep routine backup copies for a limited time before they are permanently erased.

Children

IB World FM is not made for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change we will update this page and its date. Questions: ibfmworld@gmail.com.
