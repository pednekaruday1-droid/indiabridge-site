India Bridge — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2026

India Bridge is a free directory app. It opens official websites and apps for travel, services, shopping, education, health and more. This page explains in plain words what happens to your information.

1. No account for the directory

You can use the directory without creating an account. Your favourites, language, card order and settings stay on your own phone.

2. Location

If you allow location, your phone GPS or network position is used to show your current place and nearby results. The coordinates are sent to free map services (OpenStreetMap Nominatim and BigDataCloud) only to turn them into a place name. If location is not available, the app may estimate an approximate place from your internet (IP) address using public IP-location services, which can see your IP address. We do not store your location on our servers. You can turn location off at any time in your phone settings.

3. Voice search

The mic button opens the speech recognition of your phone (usually provided by Google). India Bridge does not record or keep your voice. It only receives the text that was recognised.

4. Language

When you choose a language other than English, the words shown on the screen are sent to Google Translate to be translated. Translated text is saved on your phone so that it loads faster. Please do not rely on a translation for legal, medical or financial decisions.

When the app is in another language, the websites you open from India Bridge are opened through Google Translate so that they appear in your language. Google can then see which web page you opened. You can switch this off in Settings → Website Language. Login, payment and booking sites are never opened through Google Translate. Government, exam and university sites open inside the app: only the words of the page (not the page address and not anything you type) are sent to Google Translate.

5. Place information

For place pages, and for temple, dargah, gurudwara, church, monastery and ashram pages, the name of the place is sent to Wikipedia to fetch a short public summary and photo.

6. Bazaar (farmers marketplace)

If you post a listing, the details you type (item, quantity, price, your name, mobile number, village or city, state, country, address, and an optional photo and description) are saved in our cloud database (Google Firebase) and shown publicly so that buyers can contact you. Posting uses an anonymous sign-in, not your email. You can remove your own listings from My Listings at any time. Please post only what you are happy to share publicly. Anyone can report a listing: the report (the listing and your reason) is sent to us and the listing is hidden on your phone. We may remove listings that look fake or unsafe. A small anonymous sign-in code and the list of listings you hid are kept on your phone.

7. India Bridge Diary

The Diary is a private part of the app. If you sign up, your email and password are handled by Google Firebase Authentication, and your Diary entries are synced to your account in Google Cloud Firestore so that you can get them back on a new phone. Entries are also kept on your phone and can be protected with a PIN. Diary reminders use the alarms and notifications of your phone. You can switch them off in the Diary or in Android settings. Backups are files that you save yourself. You can choose your privacy and consent options in the Diary under Settings. To delete your Diary account and cloud data, write to us from your registered email.

8. Premium

If Premium is offered, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your card or bank details.

9. Links to other websites and apps

India Bridge opens other websites and apps, for example railway, bus, flight, shopping and government sites. Their content, bookings, payments and privacy practices belong to them, not to India Bridge. Please read their policies and take care with your personal and payment details. India Bridge is an independent directory and is not affiliated with the listed organisations.

10. Our promise

We do not sell your personal information. Data travels over secure (HTTPS) connections. Bazaar listings stay until you remove them, and Diary data stays until you delete it or ask us to delete your account.

11. Your choices

You can turn location and notifications off in Android settings, clear the app data in Android settings to remove everything stored on your phone, remove your Bazaar listings, delete your Diary entries or ask us to delete your Diary account, and choose English if you do not want text to be translated.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
