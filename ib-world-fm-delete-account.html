IB World FM — Delete your account and data

This page explains how to delete your IB World FM account (app by Grotech Global) and your cloud data, and what happens to it. You do not need an account to listen to the radio.

Last updated: November 2026

Option 1: Delete inside the app (instant)

  1. Open IB World FM and tap Settings, then Login & Firebase Sync.
  2. Sign in if you are not signed in.
  3. Type your password in the Password box and tap Delete Account. Confirm when asked.

Deleting cannot be undone.

Option 2: Ask us by email (if you uninstalled the app or cannot sign in)

Send an email to ibfmworld@gmail.com with the subject Delete my IB World FM account.

  • Write from the email address you used to sign up, or write that address in your email.
  • Do not send your password.

We delete the data within 30 days (usually much sooner) and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (email address and password).
  • Your cloud data in Google Firebase: synced favourites, custom stations, station order and settings.

What is not deleted

We do not keep a copy of deleted data. Google, our cloud provider, may hold routine backup copies for a limited time before they are permanently erased. Favourites and settings saved on your phone stay on your phone; remove them by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
